about this project

On November 11, Canadians observe Remembrance Day. This illustrated story explores whom Canadians honour, why the date matters and how the red poppy became a symbol of remembrance.

The historical information is based on publicly available materials from Veterans Affairs Canada and the Royal Canadian Legion. This independent project is not affiliated with or endorsed by either organization.
